What this is, and how to use it

A short guide to the whole library — five minutes' reading for a lifetime's shelf.

What this is

Open Materia Medica is a plain-language homeopathic materia medica: 344 remedy portraits, drawn from the public-domain classics — Boericke's manual, Clarke's Dictionary, Kent's Lectures, Allen's Keynotes, Nash's Leaders, Farrington's Clinical Materia Medica — and set beside the real toxicology and modern pharmacology of each source substance. Every remedy is one page: the classical picture set out in everyday words, the difficult terms explained, the look-alikes cross-linked, and the honest science alongside. It is compiled by Dr. Sohil Momin, BHMS, and it is not medical advice — a companion for study and reference, not a substitute for a physician.

1How to read a remedy page

Every portrait follows the same shape, so the eye learns it once. Read it top to bottom and you have met the remedy whole.

2Find a remedy, five ways

By symptom

Type any symptom, keynote, modality or substance — burning, worse midnight, fear of death, thirstless.

By name

The full index of all 344, one line each, grouped by batch.

By condition

Everyday conditions with their most-used remedies in a single line.

By rubric

The most-used rubrics of daily practice, with their first remedies.

By kingdom

Animal, vegetable, mineral, sarcode, nosode — each kingdom's signature.

By family

Snakes, spiders, the Kali family, nightshades and more — sixteen groups.

3Work with it

  • Compare two portraits — the Compare page shows any two remedies side by side in frames, opening with the classic pair, Arsenicum album and Veratrum album. The quickest way to tell look-alikes apart.
  • Print a handout — every page prints cleanly: the navigation and the search box drop away and the layout holds. Any remedy page becomes a patient handout with one Ctrl+P.
  • Carry it offline — hosted on the web, the site installs on a phone as an offline app: the whole library, no internet. Search, the evidence, references and glossary all travel with it.
  • Move with the keyboard — press / anywhere to jump to the search box, Esc to clear it, and click any remedy name to open its page.
