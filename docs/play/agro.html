Agro-homeopathy — crop disease & remedy index

The plant problems of the field, orchard and store-room: recognition signs for orientation, first remedies in a single line, from the tradition of homeopathic agriculture.

Before the remedies. This index reflects the tradition of agro-homeopathy — a practice with a devoted following and thin evidence. Crop diagnosis and protection rest on agronomy: resistant varieties, rotation, drainage, biological controls and integrated pest management. Use these tables as the tradition's own record, and let a plant pathologist or agronomist's diagnosis lead.

Working rules of the agro-homeopathic tradition

  • Prevention over cure — strengthen the plant (Silicea, soil vitality) before the pest arrives, as the tradition's first principle.
  • Small and frequent — the tradition sprays in high dilutions, repeated weekly, rather than in single heavy doses.
  • Early morning or evening — the tradition sprays when the stomata are open and the sun is off the leaf.
  • The soil is half the plant — compost, drainage and rotation come first; the remedies accompany, never replace, good husbandry.
  • Observe the field — the agro-homeopath's art is walking the crop daily, reading the leaf before the pest counts rise.
