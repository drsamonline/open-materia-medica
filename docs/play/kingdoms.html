The kingdoms

All 344 remedies of this library, arranged by the kingdom of their source — animal, vegetable, mineral, sarcode, nosode — with each kingdom's traditional signature in plain words.

A note before the kingdoms. Kingdom signatures are a traditional teaching lens of homeopathy — useful for study and memory, not evidence of how the remedies work. Some classifications vary between authors (milk remedies, for instance, appear under both animal and sarcode headings in different books); where this library differs, it says so. The science question is discussed once, neutrally, on the Evidence page.

ANIMAL 26 remedies

The creature's themes. Animal remedies carry the themes of survival: intensity, competition, cunning and strategy; sexuality and its dramas; the stalk, the sting, the bite; fear of being caught or attacked. In the old books the animal remedies run hot and passionate or cold and calculating — Lachesis talks and rivals, the spiders dance and scheme, the snakes strike and bleed, the insects sting and swarm. Themes of dominance and submission, of display and disguise, mark the chapter.

VEGETABLE 215 remedies

The plant's themes. Plant remedies carry the themes of sensitivity and growth: impressionability, reactivity, the awkwardness of the growing thing. In the old books the plant remedies are the weather-vanes — worse for damp (Dulcamara), for storms (Rhododendron), for the sun's heat; oversensitive to every influence, reacting with changeable moods (Pulsatilla), irritable rootedness (Nux), or the climbing ambition of the vines. Sensitivity without armour is the plant's signature.

MINERAL 79 remedies

The mineral's themes. Mineral remedies carry the themes of structure and security: the framework of the body and the self — bones, glands, the very lattice of life; deficiency and excess; the terror of the structure failing. In the old books the mineral remedies are the constitutional builders and preservers — the calcium salts of bone and growth, the potassium salts of nerve and mucus, the iron of the blood, the mercury of the glands; orderly, periodic, structural. The periodic table's logic — row by row, deficiency by deficiency — is the mineral chapter's quiet map.

SARCODES 6 remedies

The secretion's themes. Sarcodes — remedies from the body's own healthy secretions — carry the themes of supply and demand: the milk that nourishes (Lac caninum, Lac defloratum), the bile that digests (Fel tauri), the enzyme that unravels protein (Pepsinum), the hormone of alarm (Adrenalinum), the waste of protein burned (Urea). In the old books they serve the gland and the metabolism — what the body makes, in excess or deficiency, is the sarcode's portrait.

6 remedies in this library:

Lac caninum, Lac defloratum, Fel tauri, Pepsinum, Adrenalinum, Urea

NOSODES 10 remedies

The disease's themes. Nosodes — remedies prepared from disease itself — carry the themes of inheritance and periodicity: the family mark, the never-well-since, the complaint that returns on a clock (Pyrogenium, Cedron's kin); the suppressed and the inherited. In the old books they stand for the chronic dispositions — Psorinum the filthy and freezing, Medorrhinum the hot and hurried, Syphilinum the nightly destroyer, Tuberculinum and Bacillinum the family-marked chest — the portrait of the disease recorded as a remedy.

10 remedies in this library:

Psorinum, Tuberculinum bovinum, Bacillinum, Medorrhinum, Syphilinum, Carcinosin, Pyrogenium, Variolinum, Lyssin, Hippozaenium

IMPONDERABILIA 8 remedies

The force's themes. The imponderabilia — remedies made not from a weighable substance but from a physical force — carry the themes of power and opposition: light and dark, heat and cold, magnetism's pull, the shock of the current, the slow burn of radiation. Their portraits speak of suddenness, of powers acting at a distance, of rhythms and cycles.

  • Sol — sunlight
  • Luna — moonlight
  • Electricitas — electricity
  • X-Ray — Roentgen rays
  • Magnetis poli ambo — the whole magnet
  • Magnetis polus arcticus — the north pole
  • Magnetis polus australis — the south pole
  • Radium bromatum — radium bromide
