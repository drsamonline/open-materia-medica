The imponderabilia chapters

The eight imponderable remedies — the forces and energies — come from the same authors, whose chapters on the forces are the source of every portrait:

RemedySource chapters
SolClarke, Dictionary, "Sol" — Swan's 1880 pathogenesis and Finsen's light-therapy; Boericke, "Sol".
LunaClarke, Dictionary, "Luna" — Goullon's 1897 essay on the moon; Swan's proving of Luna 1m.
ElectricitasClarke, Dictionary, "Electricitas" — the symptoms of Caspari; Boericke, "Electricitas".
X-RayBoericke, "X-Ray" — Fincke's 1897 proving and the clinical record of radiation injury.
Magnetis poli ambo, polus arcticus, polus australisHahnemann, Materia Medica Pura (the magnet and its poles); Clarke, Dictionary, the three "Magnetis" chapters.
Radium bromatumBoericke, "Radium bromatum" — Diffenbach's proving; Clarke, Dictionary, "Radium".
