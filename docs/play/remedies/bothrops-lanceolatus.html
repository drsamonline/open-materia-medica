Bothrops lanceolatus

Made from the fer-de-lance venom — the pit viper whose peptides taught pharmacology how to lower blood pressure, and the classical remedy of bleeding and broken speech.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of bleeding with the brain involved: dark oozing blood from any opening, purple discoloration, and — the distinctive sign — broken, halting speech as if words cannot be found; the tongue feels stiff and too large. Dark bleeding with aphasic speech: that is Bothrops.

The substance, in plain words

The fer-de-lance is the pit viper of the French Antilles — the very snake whose venom peptides, studied by pharmacologists, led to the design of captopril, the first ACE-inhibitor blood-pressure drug. Antivenom against it is real, life-saving medicine.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Anxiety with the illness. Fear, restlessness, the mind clouding as the bleeding progresses.
  • Difficulty of speech. The keynote of the mind: words are lost mid-sentence; the person gropes for names.
  • Irritability. Cross, worse from light and noise, in the congested states.

The body

  • Dark fluid bleeding. The keynote: dark, fluid, non-clotting blood from any orifice — the snakebite's chemistry internalised.
  • Purple mottled skin. Livid discoloration, swelling, bruising without blow.
  • Aphasia and stiff tongue. Speech broken and hesitant, tongue heavy and protruding — the books' distinctive cerebral sign.
  • Amaurosis Bloodshot eyes, dimming vision with the congestion.
  • Worse lying and motion. The bleeding and swelling worsen with movement and recumbency.
  • Better erect and quiet. Sitting up, perfectly still, eases the cerebral and bleeding picture.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Blood and clotting, brain and speech, eyes, skin and tissues.

Where the classical picture comes from

Fer-de-lance envenomation — swelling, dark bleeding, neurological signs, broken speech — was carefully described in the Antilles; the proving of the venom carried the picture into the library, where it became the bleeding-with-aphasia remedy.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

This snake's family changed real medicine: the bradykinin-potentiating peptides of Bothrops venom led directly to captopril in 1975, the first ACE inhibitor, which transformed hypertension treatment worldwide. Antivenom for Bothrops bites is standard, life-saving tropical medicine. The fer-de-lance's chemistry is pharmaceutical history.
