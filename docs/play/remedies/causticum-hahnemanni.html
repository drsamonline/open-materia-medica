Causticum

Made by Hahnemann himself from slaked lime and bisulphide of sulphur — his 'burned' preparation, and the classical voice of injustice and gradual paralysis.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of the person who cannot bear injustice — weeps at others' suffering, burns with sympathy for the wronged — while the body slowly stiffens: hoarse voice worse in the morning, a creeping paralysis of face or bladder, warts on the fingers, and the odd relief of chronic complaints in damp weather. Sympathy with injustice, morning hoarseness, slow paralysis: that is Causticum.

The substance, in plain words

Hahnemann's Causticum was his own preparation — a solution of bisulphide of sulphur in slaked lime, evaporated to a semi-solid. It is essentially a preparation of calcium polysulphides — chemically a sulphur-lime compound, and the only major remedy that is the founder's private chemistry.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Sympathy and injustice. The keynote of the mind: intense feeling for others' suffering, anger at injustice, weeping at the fate of the persecuted.
  • Anxious foreboding. Fear something bad will happen, worse evening and night; dread of the future.
  • Restless melancholy. Gloom with fidgetiness; long-suffering temperament worn down by care.

The body

  • Morning hoarseness. The famous symptom: voice lost or hoarse on waking, improving as the day goes on.
  • Gradual paralysis. Slowly advancing loss of power — face, eyelids, throat, bladder, rectum — with stiffness and contraction.
  • Urinary and stool retention. Weak bladder, involuntary urine on coughing; stubborn constipation with inactivity of the rectum.
  • Warts and skin growths. The classical wart remedy, especially warts near the nails and on the fingers.
  • Throat raw and tickling. Raw, scraped sensation; cough better for cold drinks
  • Better in damp weather. A striking modality: chronic rheumatic and paralytic complaints ease in wet weather — the reverse of most remedies.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Nervous system (motor paralysis), larynx and voice, urinary organs, skin (warts), throat.

Where the classical picture comes from

The preparation's alkaline-sulphur chemistry irritated nerves and mucous membranes in provings; the paralysis picture grew from the tonic-spasmodic action the old clinicians observed. Its mental keynote of injustice came straight from the provers' emotional storms.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

The calcium-sulphur chemistry of Causticum belongs to the same family as the sulphide preparations of the old pharmacopoeias — irritant, keratolytic, antiparasitic. The conditions it portrayed — progressive hoarseness, facial palsy, bladder weakness — are today the territory of neurology and ENT medicine, where such symptoms always deserve proper diagnosis.

Safety. Hoarseness lasting more than three weeks, progressive weakness, or loss of bladder control need medical evaluation — these are symptoms that must be diagnosed, not managed at home.
