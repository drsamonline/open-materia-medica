Coffea cruda

Made from raw coffee — the unroasted bean of the world's favourite stimulant, and the classical picture of a mind that will not switch off.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of a mind over-lit: sleepless from sheer excitement — good news, new ideas, an argument replayed — senses sharpened to pain, hearing acute, thoughts racing faster than sleep can catch. Sleeplessness from excitement, acute senses, racing thoughts: that is Coffea cruda.

The substance, in plain words

Coffee is the seed of a red-berried tree, and its stimulant is caffeine — the most widely consumed psychoactive substance on earth. The homeopathic picture is drawn not from poisoning but from the ordinary over-stimulation everyone knows: one cup too many, after midnight.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Sleeplessness from excitement. The keynote: cannot sleep from an over-joyful or over-active mind — plans, ideas, the day's events replayed at speed.
  • Over-acute senses. Hearing, smell and touch unbearably sharp; the slightest noise jars; pain felt more than it should be.
  • Racing, fidgeting thought. One idea chases another; fidgets, talks fast, laughs easily, weeps at music.

The body

  • Wired but tired. Sleepless yet not sleepy — the exhausted body carried by a spinning mind.
  • Headaches of over-stimulation. Aching, bursting headache as if the brain were too tight, worse for noise and thinking.
  • Palpitations with excitement. Heart fluttering after joy, coffee or surprise; trembling of the hands.
  • Toothache made sharp. Pains intolerable — 'pains are unbearable' is the quoted phrase of the proving.
  • Worse for coffee. The substance aggravates its own picture — coffee after dinner, no sleep.
  • Better for warmth and quiet. Warmth, darkness and stillness gradually let the senses settle.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Mind and sleep, senses and nerves, heart, teeth and head.

Where the classical picture comes from

The proving with raw coffee produced the classic caffeine state — excitation, acute senses, sleeplessness — and the remedy was then applied to the same state arising from any cause: joy, grief, overwork of the brain.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Caffeine is genuine, dose-dependent pharmacology: a stimulant that blocks adenosine receptors, sharpening attention at one dose and wrecking sleep at another. Modern sleep medicine manages exactly the state Coffea portrays — with sleep hygiene, and an honest look at the last coffee of the day.

Safety. Sleeplessness with chest pain, breathlessness, or days without sleep needs medical review; severe caffeine overuse can trigger palpitations.
