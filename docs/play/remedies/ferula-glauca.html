Ferula glauca

Made from the giant fennel's blue-green resin — the asafoetida family's European cousin, and the classical remedy of the tobacco intolerance.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of a frame that cannot bear tobacco: the vomiting and headache at the smell or taste of tobacco, the books' ferula chapter of the resin family, and the blue-green fennel of the Mediterranean hills. The tobacco-intolerant resin: that is Ferula glauca.

The substance, in plain words

Ferula glauca is the blue-green giant fennel of the Mediterranean hills, its resin kin to the asafoetida of this library — the same genuine gum-resin family, its pungent sulfur chemistry really acting on the stomach. The proving gave the resin the tobacco-intolerance portrait.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Aversion with the nausea. The disgust and dread of the tobacco smell, worse at night.
  • Irritability. Cross from the constant nausea and headache.
  • Anxiety about health. Worry over the intolerance, the headaches, the stomach.

The body

  • Vomiting on the smell of tobacco. The keynote: the nausea and vomiting at the smell or taste of tobacco — the books' celebrated characteristic.
  • Headache of the intolerance. The headache that rides the tobacco contact.
  • Worse tobacco and rich food. The smell and the rich meal aggravate.
  • Better for fresh air and rest. Fresh air and rest ease the ferula state.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Where the classical picture comes from

The resin family's proving gave the tobacco-intolerance portrait; Ferula glauca became the classical remedy of the frame that cannot bear tobacco — the blue-green fennel serving the intolerance its pungent chemistry mirrors.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

The ferula family's resins are genuinely active — the asafoetida chemistry that moves the wind and turns the stomach, documented across the family's long career. The tobacco intolerance it portrayed is now understood as real sensitivity; the resin's chemistry is honest, its portrait a mirror of its own pungency.
