Hamamelis virginiana

Made from witch hazel — the North American shrub whose distilled bark is on every modern pharmacy shelf, and the classical remedy of veins and bruised blood.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of blood that bruises and veins that ache: varicose veins that feel bruised and beaten, bleeding piles that smart, passive dark oozing from broken surfaces, and a beaten-soreness of the whole venous system. Bruised veins, passive bleeding: that is Hamamelis.

The substance, in plain words

Witch hazel is the North American shrub whose bark and leaves carry tannins — the astringent chemistry that contracts tissue and stanches ooze. Distilled witch hazel is on every modern pharmacy shelf as a skin protectant; the remedy is the classical portrait of the whole bruised venous system.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Weariness with the pains. Tired, worn, wants to lie down with the bruised-vein states.
  • Irritability. Crossness in the evening with the aching limbs.
  • Anxiety about health. Worry in the long varicose and bleeding troubles.

The body

  • Bruised, aching veins. The keynote: varicose veins that feel as if bruised or beaten, legs heavy and sore.
  • Bleeding piles. Piles that smart, itch and bleed; the books' first rectal remedy.
  • Passive dark bleeding. Dark, oozing, non-spurting blood from nose, gums, wounds — venous bleeding.
  • Phlebitis picture. Hard, tender, inflamed veins after injury; sore cords under the skin.
  • Bruises with soreness. Bruises that stay sore and blue; sprains with venous engorgement.
  • Worse heat and pressure Heat and pressure aggravate the veins; cool ease.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Veins and venous circulation, rectum (piles), blood (passive bleeding), bruised tissues.

Where the classical picture comes from

Native American medicine taught the colonists witch hazel for bruises and bleeding; the proving confirmed the tannin astringency picture, and the remedy became the classical portrait of the overloaded, bruised venous system.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Witch hazel distillate is a genuine, FDA-recognised skin protectant sold worldwide for minor bleeding, irritation and haemorrhoidal discomfort — its tannin astringency is real pharmacology, not folklore. The shrub's chemistry never left the pharmacy; it merely moved shelves.
