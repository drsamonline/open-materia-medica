Acidum hydrocyanicum

Made from hydrocyanic acid — the prussic acid of the laurel family, with a genuine emergency antidote in every modern department, and the classical remedy of the convulsive, clonic spasm.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of a spasm that alternates and locks: convulsions of the clonic-tonic type — relaxation and rigidity alternating — with the books' use in the spastic, twitching states, the weak, irregular heart of the cyanide shadow, and the terrible chemistry of the same molecule that sits in the cherry-laurel hedge. The alternating convulsion: that is Hydrocyanic acid.

The substance, in plain words

Hydrocyanic acid is prussic acid — the cyanide chemistry of the stone-fruit pits and the cherry laurel, one of the fastest poisons known, with a genuine, specific antidote (hydroxocobalamin) stocked in modern emergency care. The proving gave the remedy its convulsive, cardiac portrait.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Anxiety and fear. Fear of death, of the spasm, worse at night.
  • Irritability. Cross, worse from noise and light.
  • Despondency. Gloomy in the chronic weak states.

The body

  • Alternating clonic convulsions. The keynote: convulsions in which relaxation and rigidity alternate — the clonic-tonic spasm of the books.
  • Weak irregular heart. The cardiac shadow — feeble, irregular action, the cyanide's mirror.
  • Spastic and twitching states. The remedy of the books' spastic chapter — twitches, jerks, the neuro-muscular irritability.
  • Worse cold damp and night. Wet weather and night deepen the spasms and the weakness.
  • Gasping respiration The breathing falters with the heart — the toxicology's pattern.
  • Better for rest and quiet. Perfect stillness and quiet ease the threatened system.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Nervous system (convulsions, spasm), heart and circulation, respiration.

Where the classical picture comes from

The cyanide chemistry's speed and terror gave the proving its convulsive, cardiac portrait; Hydrocyanic acid became the classical remedy of the alternating spasm and the weak irregular heart, read beside the laurel hedge that carries the same molecule.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Hydrocyanic acid is genuinely one of the fastest poisons — cellular respiration poisoned at the mitochondria — and its antidote, hydroxocobalamin, is real, stocked and life-saving in modern emergency departments. Cyanide's chemistry is also genuinely useful: industrial processes, laboratory synthesis, even the bitter-almond flavour chemistry of food. The poison and the antidote are both real, and both modern.
