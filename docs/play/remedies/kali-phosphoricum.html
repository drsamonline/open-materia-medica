Kali phosphoricum

Made from potassium phosphate — the nerve-salt of the old biochemic system, and the classical remedy of mental prostration.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of a mind run dry: the student or clerk after weeks of overwork — grey exhaustion, slighted worry, dread of the future, sleeplessness from anxious fatigue, memory failing just when needed most. Brain-fag: that one old word is the whole of Kali phosphoricum.

The substance, in plain words

Potassium phosphate is one of the body's essential phosphate salts — part of the chemistry of every nerve impulse and energy transfer. The old biochemic school made it their 'nerve salt'; whether or not the salt can do in dilution what diet does in bulk, its chemistry is genuinely the chemistry of nerve work.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Mental prostration. The keynote: brain-fag — exhaustion of the mind from over-study, overwork or anxiety; thought becomes an effort.
  • Anxious dread. Groundless fear of bad news, of death, of the future; startles easily; slightest worry exhausts.
  • Irritable exhaustion. Cross from weakness, not from anger; children become fretful and study-shy.

The body

  • Sleeplessness from fatigue. Tired beyond sleep — the body worn out, the mind still nibbling at worries.
  • Failing memory. Names and words slip away; comprehension lags; the student blank at the examination.
  • Headaches of students. Aching, empty, bruised feeling in the head, worse for the least mental effort.
  • Nervous dyspepsia. Gas, sinking at the stomach, poor appetite with the mental fatigue.
  • Numbness and weakness. Pains with numbness in parts; back gives out; general lowered vitality.
  • Worse for exertion. Everything — mood, sleep, memory, digestion — sags further with effort, recovers with rest.

Where the classical picture comes from

The biochemic school of Schuessler gave Kali-phos its modern form — the idea that a 'nerve salt' could restore a depleted nervous system. Classical prescribers had already described the brain-fag picture from clinical experience with the overworked; the remedy united both streams.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Phosphate chemistry is the real engine of nerve and energy metabolism — ATP, the cell's energy currency, is adenosine triphosphate — and potassium is an essential electrolyte. Genuine deficiency of either is a medical matter of diet and disease, not dilution; the modern treatment of burnout and anxiety is rest, psychology and, where needed, medicine.
