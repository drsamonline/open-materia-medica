Mandragora

Made from the mandrake root — the man-shaped root of legend, the tropane alkaloids of the nightshade family, and the classical remedy of the rheumatic and the ulcerated.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of the legendary root's real portrait: rheumatic pains with the contraction of the muscles, the ulcerated stomach of the old books, the dryness and the delirium of the family, and the books' great mandrake of the nightshade chapter. The legendary-root remedy: that is Mandragora.

The substance, in plain words

The mandrake is the man-shaped root of Mediterranean legend — the root that shrieked when pulled, the witch's familiar of every old tale. Its chemistry is genuinely the nightshade family's: tropane alkaloids, kin to belladonna's atropine and henbane's scopolamine, real and dangerous. The proving gave the legend its clinical portrait.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Delirium of the family. The nightshade family's mental chapter — confusion, delusion, the books' mandrake shading.
  • Anxiety and fear. Fear of death, worse at night.
  • Irritability. Cross, worse from contradiction and the pains.

The body

  • Rheumatic contraction. The keynote: rheumatic pains with the contraction of the muscles and tendons — the family's motor shading.
  • Ulcerated stomach. The books' great use — the ulcerated, painful stomach of the old chapter.
  • Dryness of the family. The nightshade dryness — mouth, throat, skin.
  • Worse cold damp and night. Wet weather and night deepen the rheumatism and the ulcer.
  • Better for warmth and rest. Warmth and rest ease the contracted, ulcerated frame.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Muscles and joints (rheumatic contraction), stomach (ulcerated), mouth and throat, mind.

Where the classical picture comes from

The shrieking root of legend proved a real portrait: rheumatic contraction, the ulcerated stomach, the family's dryness and delirium; Mandragora became the classical remedy of the nightshade chapter's legendary member — the witch's root recorded by the old school.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

The mandrake's tropane alkaloids are genuinely the family's — atropine and scopolamine's kin, the chemistry of belladonna and henbane, real, dangerous and still studied. The plant of every old tale carries the same molecules as the era's greatest drugs; the legends were wrong about the shriek but right about the power. The ulcer it portrayed is now gastroenterology's, and real treatment exists.
