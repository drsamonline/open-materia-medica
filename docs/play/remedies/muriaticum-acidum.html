Acidum muriaticum

Made from hydrochloric acid — the stomach's own acid, the chemistry of digestion itself, and the classical remedy of the protruding, powerless piles and the bedridden weakness.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of strength draining out through the lower parts: hemorrhoids so weak they protrude and cannot be drawn back, blue and bleeding, in a person too exhausted to rise from bed; ulcerations that spread; the weakness of typhoid's aftermath; and the acid that the stomach itself makes as the remedy. The protruding-piles, bedridden-weakness remedy: that is Muriatic acid.

The substance, in plain words

Hydrochloric acid is the stomach's own gastric juice — the chemistry of digestion itself, secreted by every healthy stomach lining. Concentrated, it dissolves metal; diluted, it once treated dyspepsia; the proving gave the remedy its portrait of the lowest, weakest states — the parts that give way, the strength that drains.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Irritability and weakness. Cross from sheer exhaustion, too weak to argue; the books paint the bedridden temperament.
  • Anxiety and fear. Fear of dying, worse at night, in the low states.
  • Indifference. A flat, don't-care apathy with the weakness.

The body

  • Protruding, powerless piles. The keynote: hemorrhoids so relaxed they protrude constantly, blue, bleeding, too weak to retract.
  • Bedridden weakness. Too exhausted to rise — the books' great typhoid-convalescent and low-fever remedy.
  • Ulcers spreading. Ulcers of the mouth, skin, genitals that spread and refuse to heal, with offensive discharge.
  • Worse damp cold and night. Wet weather and night deepen the weakness and the ulceration.
  • After typhoid and fevers. The remedy of the convalescent who cannot convalesce — strength gone through the lowest parts.
  • Better for rest Warmth and quiet support the failing powers.

Where the classical picture comes from

The acid of the stomach's own making proved the portrait of its absence — the lowest parts giving way, the strength draining. Muriatic acid became the remedy of the protruding piles and the bedridden convalescent, read beside the physiology of digestion it belongs to.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Hydrochloric acid's real medicine is physiology itself: gastric acid digests, sterilises and signals; its deficiency (atrophic gastritis, PPI overuse) is genuine modern gastroenterology. Concentrated HCl is a genuine industrial hazard — corrosive, regulated, real. The remedy's territory — prolapse, ulceration, exhaustion — is today surgery, wound care and rehabilitation's.
