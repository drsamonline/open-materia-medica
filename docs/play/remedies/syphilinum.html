Syphilinum

The classical nosode prepared from syphilis — the disease that shadowed every clinic of the old world, conquered by penicillin, and the classical remedy of the slow, destructive, ulcerating constitution.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of a constitution that destroys itself slowly: relentless nightly pains that drive the sufferer from bed, ulcers that corrode without healing, a history of the disease and its bad mercury years, despair that deepens with the darkness — and the nightly aggravation as the signature of the whole. The slow, destructive nosode: that is Syphilinum.

The substance, in plain words

Syphilinum is prepared from the syphilitic virus of the pre-penicillin era — the disease that shadowed every nineteenth-century clinic and filled the casebooks with its mercury-scarred history. Its nosode is the era's portrait of the inherited and acquired legacy, written before the spirochaete was even seen.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Deepening despair. The keynote of the mind: gloom that sinks with the sun, hopelessness, the feeling of being doomed — worse at night.
  • Irritability. Cross, worse at night, with the pains and the darkness.
  • Fear of the disease. A fixed dread of the illness itself, of its return, of its legacy.

The body

  • Nightly pains that drive from bed. The keynote: pains so relentless at night that the sufferer must rise and walk — the books' most quoted sign.
  • Ulcers that corrode. Punched-out, corroding, non-healing ulcers of the legacy — ragged, destructive, chronic.
  • History of the disease and mercury. The old books take the history — the infection, the years of mercury, the suppressions — as the ground of the case.
  • Worse at night. Everything — pains, gloom, the ulcers' burning — climbs with the darkness.
  • Bone and periosteal pains. Deep, boring, nightly bone-pains of the old books' syphilitic-rheumatic chapter.
  • Better for rest Motion and the daylight bring the only ease; the night is the enemy.

Where the classical picture comes from

The syphilitic era wrote the nosode: the mercury years, the suppressed discharges, the nightly bone-pains and corroding ulcers — the whole legacy of the disease that penicillin would erase. Syphilinum is that era's faithful portrait, kept in the library as history.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Syphilis is a real spirochaetal infection — today diagnosed by blood test and cured with penicillin, a single course, one of medicine's absolute victories. Its untreated history — the nightly bone-pains, the corroding ulcers, the madness — is now a chapter in medical museums, and the congenital form prevented by screening. The disease the nosode portrayed is, today, curable.
