Variolinum

The classical nosode prepared from smallpox — the disease whose eradication is medicine's greatest achievement, and the classical remedy of the pustular fever and its dread.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of the pustular fever and its long shadow: fever with backache and the creeping eruption, the dread of the disfiguring pox, the books' use in the fever's course and in the sequelae that scarred — the portrait of the disease that shaped vaccination itself. The pox and its dread: that is Variolinum.

The substance, in plain words

Variolum is prepared from the smallpox matter itself — the disease that killed millions and scarred more, against which Jenner's vaccination, announced in 1796, was medicine's first great triumph. The nosode belongs to the era of the pox; the disease it records was, within living memory, the terror of every household on earth.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Dread of the disease. The keynote of the mind: fear of the disfigurement, of the death, of the household's ruin — the terror the old books knew by heart.
  • Anxiety and restlessness. Worry with the fever; restlessness during the eruption.
  • Irritability. Cross, worse from light and noise — the fevered intolerance.

The body

  • Fever with backache and eruption. The keynote: the pox's own course — fever, backache, the creeping eruption the books describe in detail.
  • The books' use in the course. The old books used the nosode during the fever and in the scarred sequelae.
  • Worse heat and light. Warmth and light aggravate — the pox's intolerance.
  • Sequelae of scarring. The pits and scars the disease left — the books' long shadow.
  • Worse night. The fever and the dread climb at night.
  • Better for cool air and rest. Cool, dark quiet — what the fevered pox patient needed.

Where the classical picture comes from

The pox that shaped vaccination gave the nosode its portrait; Variolinum was the old books' answer to the disease and its dread — written in the era when smallpox was still the terror of every household, and vaccination still young.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Smallpox is medicine's supreme achievement: Jenner's 1796 vaccination began the conquest, and in 1980 the World Health Organisation declared the disease eradicated — the first, and still the only, human disease deliberately wiped from the earth. The pox the nosode recorded is now a museum piece, kept alive only in two laboratory freezers. No chapter in this library has a more triumphant ending.
