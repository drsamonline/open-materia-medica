Vipera berus

Made from the adder's venom — the viper of the European heath whose bite antivenom answers, and the classical remedy of the swollen, bursting vein.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of a vein swollen to bursting: painful phlebitis with the vein standing out like a cord, bursting pains on letting the limb hang down, varicose veins that throb and swell, the blue-shiny skin over the engorged vessel — the books' great venous-engorgement remedy. The bursting-vein remedy: that is Vipera.

The substance, in plain words

The adder is the only venomous snake of most of Europe, its bite a genuine medical emergency answered by real antivenom — and its venom, kin to all snake venoms, acts on vessels and clotting in ways modern haemostasis studies closely. The proving gave the remedy the swollen-vein portrait.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Anxiety and fear. Fear of the swelling, of the bursting, worse at night.
  • Irritability. Cross, worse from contradiction and from the constant pain.
  • Despondency. Gloomy in the chronic venous states.

The body

  • Bursting pains, vein engorged. The keynote: pains as if the vein would burst — phlebitis with the vessel standing out like a cord.
  • Worse letting the limb hang. The quoted modality — hanging the limb down brings the bursting pain; elevation relieves.
  • Varicose veins. The throbbing, swollen varices of the books — the remedy's great venous territory.
  • Blue, shiny skin over the vessel. The books describe the blue-shiny stretched skin of the engorged vein.
  • Worse cold damp and motion. Wet weather and movement of the part aggravate.
  • Better for elevation and rest. Raising the limb and rest ease the engorgement.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Veins (phlebitis, varices), skin over the vessels, circulation, general vitality.

Where the classical picture comes from

The adder's proving gave the bursting-vein portrait; Vipera became the classical remedy of the engorged, corded vein — the European viper's venom recorded in the vessel it swells.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Adder venom is a genuine haemotoxic and cytotoxic venom — the bite a real emergency with real antivenom — and snake venoms broadly are a serious research field for clotting, vessels and pain. The phlebitis it portrayed is now vascular medicine's: ultrasound-diagnosed, anticoagulated, and dangerous when ignored. The venom is real; its portrait faithful.
