Yohimbinum

Made from the bark of the yohimbe tree — the African aphrodisiac of legend whose alkaloid is a genuine, receptor-precise drug of modern pharmacology.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of a vitality that will not rise: sexual weakness with the exhaustion of the books, the cold, relaxed parts, the dragging lumbar aches, and the portrait of the depleted nervous system behind the legend. The will-not-rise remedy: that is Yohimbinum.

The substance, in plain words

The yohimbe is the West African tree whose bark made the famous love-potions of legend. Its alkaloid yohimbine is genuinely a precise, modern pharmacological tool: an alpha-2 adrenergic antagonist with a documented mechanism, studied and prescribed for specific weaknesses of the circulation and the will.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Anxiety and despondency. Worry over the weakness, the gloom of the depleted state, worse at night.
  • Irritability. Cross from sheer depletion, the books' portrait of the exhausted nerve.
  • Dullness. Heavy mind with the chronic exhaustion.

The body

  • Sexual weakness. The keynote: the weakness and relaxation of the parts of the books' chapter — the bark's legendary territory.
  • Lumbar dragging and exhaustion. The dragging backache and exhaustion accompanying the depletion.
  • Coldness and relaxation. The cold, relaxed, unresponsive frame of the proving.
  • Weak circulation. The books' shaded circulation — the modern echo of the alkaloid's vascular action.
  • Worse exertion and night. Effort and the night deepen the depletion.
  • Better for rest and warmth. Rest, warmth and time restore what the depletion took.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Sexual organs, circulation, lumbar region, general vitality.

Where the classical picture comes from

The love-potion bark's proving gave the will-not-rise portrait; Yohimbinum became the classical remedy of the depleted weakness behind the legend — the bark of the love-potions recorded with more clinical honesty than the potions themselves.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Yohimbine is genuinely one of pharmacology's most precise alkaloids: an alpha-2 adrenergic antagonist with a documented mechanism of action, studied for orthostatic hypotension and specific vascular weaknesses, and the subject of real clinical research. The legend's chemistry turned out to be a receptor-precise drug — the potion had a pharmacology all along.
