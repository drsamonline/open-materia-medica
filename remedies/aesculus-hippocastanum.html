Aesculus hippocastanum

Made from horse chestnut — the avenue tree whose seed extract, escin, is a genuine venotonic drug of modern European pharmacy, and the classical remedy of the purple, backaching piles.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of a rectum purple and dry: hemorrhoids that are purple, swollen and intensely painful, with a characteristic dry, aching fullness in the small of the back; burning, rawness and dryness as if the rectum were full of small sticks. The purple-piles-with-backache remedy: that is Aesculus.

The substance, in plain words

The horse chestnut is the great avenue tree of Europe, its shiny seed carrying escin — a genuine venotonic saponin, extracted and sold as a real, licensed drug for venous insufficiency and haemorrhoids in European pharmacies to this day. The proving gave the remedy the same portrait the extract serves.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Anxiety and irritability. Cross, worried, worse in the damp and during the rectal suffering.
  • Despondency. Gloomy in the chronic venous states.
  • Dullness. Heavy mind with the congestive complaints.

The body

  • Purple, swollen, painful piles. The keynote: hemorrhoids purple, tender, swollen — the books' great pile remedy.
  • Dry, aching fullness of the back. The characteristic accompaniment — a dry, lame, aching small-of-back.
  • Rawness, burning and dryness. The rectum feels raw, burned, and full of small sticks — the quoted sensations.
  • Venous congestion generally. Varicose veins, purple congestion of the portal system — the escin territory.
  • Worse cold damp and standing. Wet weather and standing aggravate the congestion.
  • Better for rest and cool air Rest, cool applications and lying ease the congestion.

Modalities (worse and better factors)

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Rectum and piles, veins and portal circulation, back, general venous system.

Where the classical picture comes from

The old pile remedy's proving gave the purple-dry-backaching portrait; Aesculus became the classical companion of the venous-congested constitution, standing beside its own seed's real career in the modern pharmacy.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Escin — horse chestnut seed extract — is a genuine, licensed venotonic drug in European medicine: standardised extracts are prescribed and sold for chronic venous insufficiency and haemorrhoidal complaints, with clinical evidence for symptom relief. The tree's remedy and the tree's drug serve the same portrait, two centuries apart — one of the library's cleanest source-to-medicine lines.
