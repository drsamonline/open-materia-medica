Acidum benzoicum

Made from benzoic acid — the urinary antiseptic of the old pharmacopoeias, still present in medicine as sodium benzoate, and the classical remedy of strong-smelling urine with gout.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of urine that announces itself: dark, hot, offensive-smelling urine that irritates everything it passes; joint pains that shift with the bladder trouble; and the combination — gouty joints and urinary symptoms together — that the old books called the benzoic constitution. Strong-smelling urine with wandering joints: that is Benzoicum acidum.

The substance, in plain words

Benzoic acid is the preservative chemistry of berries and resins — official as a urinary antiseptic of the old pharmacopoeias, given when the urine needed disinfecting, and still present in medicine as sodium benzoate for metabolic disorders. The proving gave the remedy its urinary-gout portrait.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Dullness and heaviness. Foggy, heavy-minded with the bladder and joint states; the books note the dull uraemic shade.
  • Anxiety about health. Worry over the returning attacks.
  • Irritability. Cross, worse in damp weather.

The body

  • Offensive, strong-smelling urine. The keynote: dark, hot, ammoniacal urine whose smell fills the room — the books' most quoted urinary sign.
  • Gouty joints with urinary symptoms. The pairing: wandering articular pains that come and go with the bladder trouble.
  • Irritation of the passages. Burning and smarting at every passage, the urine hot and acrid.
  • Asthma association. The books add the respiratory link — the gouty, urinary, asthmatic constitution.
  • Worse damp cold and night. Wet weather and night aggravate both systems.
  • Better for rest Warmth and rest between attacks ease both the joints and the urine.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Urinary tract (bladder, urethra, kidneys), joints (gouty), respiration (asthmatic link), urine.

Where the classical picture comes from

The official urinary antiseptic's career — disinfecting the urine when infection meant suffering — gave the proving its frame; the remedy became the classical portrait of the gouty-urinary-asthmatic constitution, its strong-smelling urine the most quoted sign in the urinary chapter.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Benzoic acid was a genuine urinary antiseptic of the pharmacopoeias, and its sodium salt remains a real medicine — used in metabolic disorders of the urea cycle to this day. The gout it accompanied is now managed with allopurinol and urate-lowering drugs, among the most effective of modern therapeutics; the urine it disinfected is now antibiotics after culture.
