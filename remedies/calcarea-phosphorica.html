Calcarea phosphorica

Made from calcium phosphate — the mineral that builds every bone, and the classical remedy of the growing child and the slow-healing adult.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of growth that can't keep up: children slow to walk, slow to teeth, thin and puny with big heads and soft bones; adults whose fractures heal slowly and whose blood runs thin. The growth-salt: that is Calcarea phosphorica.

The substance, in plain words

Calcium phosphate is hydroxyapatite — the very mineral that builds bone and teeth. The biochemic school made it the salt of growth and repair; the chemistry needs no exaggeration: every skeleton on earth is, in large part, this salt arranged by cells.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Low spirits and peevishness. Peevish, fretful, discontented children; adults gloomy with the slow healing.
  • Anxiety about health. Fear of weakness, of never growing straight.
  • Forgetfulness. Slow mental development alongside slow physical.

The body

  • Slow dentition and delayed walking. The keynote: children late at every milestone — teeth late, walking late, fontanelles late to close.
  • Thin, puny, large-headed children. The classical rachitic silhouette: big head, thin neck, pot-belly, wasted limbs.
  • Slow fracture healing. In adults, the broken bone that knits slowly; non-union's companion.
  • Anaemic, thin blood. The blood runs thin and watery — the books pair it with chlorosis.
  • Worse damp and cold. Growth and healing both suffer in wet weather; aches in the cold.
  • Better in summer. Like Calc-carb, the whole constitution brightens in warm, dry months.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Bones and teeth (growth), blood (anaemia), glands, general development.

Where the classical picture comes from

The biochemic growth-salt entered the proving as its chemistry dictates: bones, teeth, blood. The remedy became the classical companion of rickets and delayed development — a portrait of calcium-starved childhood before nutrition science named it.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Calcium phosphate's real medicine needs no defence: it is bone. Rickets, the disease this portrait photographed, was conquered by vitamin D and calcium — one of the twentieth century's cleanest nutritional victories. The remedy's territory is now paediatric nutrition and orthopaedics, and it is entirely curable.
