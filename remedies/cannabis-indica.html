Cannabis indica

Made from Indian hemp — the plant whose THC chemistry is genuinely pharmacological and genuinely medical, and the classical remedy of the time-and-space distortion.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of a mind unmoored from time and space: minutes stretching like hours, the body feeling enormous or tiny, sounds and colours overwhelming, an unquenchable thirst with a dry mouth, the books' great remedy of the hashish-intoxication picture applied to the disordered nervous system. The unmoored mind: that is Cannabis indica.

The substance, in plain words

Indian hemp is the cannabis plant of Asia, its THC one of the most-studied psychoactive molecules in pharmacology — genuinely active at the brain's own cannabinoid receptors, and the source of real, licensed medicines. The proving gave the remedy its portrait of the time-and-space distortion, written in the era of the hashish experiments of the French Romantic writers.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Distortion of time and space. The keynote of the mind: minutes pass like hours; distances alter; the self feels enormous or shrunken — the books' celebrated hashish picture.
  • Overwhelming senses. Sounds seem to come from far away; light is too bright; every sense exaggerated.
  • Anxiety and fear. Fear of losing the mind, worse in the quiet, under the distortion.

The body

  • Dry mouth with unquenchable thirst. The keynote: a dry, sticky mouth with a thirst nothing quenches.
  • Distorted bodily sensations. The body feels too large or too small, parts swollen or distant — the books' catalogue of the distortion.
  • Urinary and uterine association The books add the urinary irritation of the proving.
  • Worse the quiet and the dark. Solitude and darkness deepen the distortion — company and light steady it.
  • Headaches of the hashish type The congestive, bursting headache of the proving.
  • Better for company and open air. Company, light and fresh air — the quoted ameliorations of the unmoored state.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Where the classical picture comes from

The hashish-intoxication experiments of the 1840s gave the proving its frame — Moreau de Tours' 'Hashish and Mental Alienation' and the Romantics' Club des Haschischins; Cannabis indica became the classical remedy of the distorted mind, the books' record of the era's great self-experiment.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

THC is genuinely pharmacological: the discovery of the brain's own cannabinoid receptors and endocannabinoids transformed neuroscience, and THC-derived medicines are real — dronabinol treats chemotherapy nausea and wasting, cannabidiol treats certain epilepsies, and the plant's medical chemistry is a genuine, regulated, growing field. The proving's distortion picture is the drug's own pharmacology, catalogued a century before the receptor was found.
