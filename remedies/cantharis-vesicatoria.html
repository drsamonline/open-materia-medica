Cantharis vesicatoria

Made from the Spanish fly — the blister beetle whose cantharidin is now, remarkably, an approved modern drug for molluscum contagiosum.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of burning on urination so violent that the person screams before the stream starts: constant urging, urine passed drop by drop with cutting pain, a burned raw feeling in the bladder and urethra, sometimes with blood. The same burning blistering force appears on skin and in the mind as furious restlessness. Burning, cutting, constant-urging: that is Cantharis.

The substance, in plain words

The blister beetle carries cantharidin, a blistering agent so reliable that powdered 'Spanish fly' was a pharmaceutical standard for raising blisters for two centuries. Cantharidin's real pharmacology — blistering the epidermis — has now returned as an approved medicine for a viral skin disease.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Furious, amorous excitement. The proving produced intense mental and sexual excitement, rage, mania with biting and tearing.
  • Restlessness with pain. Constant motion, crying out before and during urination.
  • Anxiety in acute states. Fear of death, convulsive sobbing in the violent inflammatory complaints.

The body

  • Burning cutting urination. The keynote cluster: intolerable urging, urine in drops, scalding, cutting before, during and after passing; the classical cystitis remedy.
  • Bladder spasm. Tenesmus of the bladder — urging returns the moment urination ends.
  • Burns and scalds. The books' first remedy for burns — raw, smarting, blistering surfaces.
  • Blistering skin eruptions. Vesicles that burn and sting like fire; erysipelas-like states with blistering.
  • Bloody urine and retention. Passage of blood and debris; retention with spasm in children and the aged.
  • Gastrointestinal burning. Burning thirst, vomiting of blood-streaked mucus, violent inflammation after drinking coffee.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Urinary tract (bladder and urethra), skin (burns, blisters), gastrointestinal mucosa, genital organs.

Where the classical picture comes from

Cantharidin is a violent irritant of the urinary tract — poisonings gave the world the exact picture of the remedy: scalding, drops of bloody urine, blistering and frenzy. The proving simply confirmed the toxicology.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Cantharidin never left real medicine: blister plasters used it for centuries, and in 2023 a cantharidin solution (Ycanth) was approved as the first treatment for molluscum contagiosum, a common viral skin infection of children. The blister beetle's chemistry, in controlled topical doses, is genuine modern pharmacology.
