Cinnabaris

Made from cinnabar — the crimson ore of mercury, the vermilion of emperors' scrolls, and the classical remedy of the syphilitic throat and the red-blotted skin.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of the mercury family's red twin: throat complaints of the old syphilitic chapter, ulcerated gums and mouth, red blotches and coppery spots of the skin, the whole crimson-ore portrait of the mercury-iodide shelf. The crimson-ore remedy: that is Cinnabaris.

The substance, in plain words

Cinnabar is the crimson ore of mercury — mercury sulfide, the vermilion pigment of emperors' scrolls and temples across Asia, genuinely toxic in its mercury and genuinely beautiful in its red. The proving gave the ore its throat-and-skin portrait, the mercury family in its crimson form.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Anxiety and fear. Fear of the disease, of the throat, worse at night.
  • Irritability. Cross, worse from contradiction and the constant discomfort.
  • Despondency. Gloomy in the chronic, recurrent states.

The body

  • Syphilitic-throat chapter. The keynote of use: the throat complaints of the old syphilitic chapter — ulcerated, obstinate, recurrent.
  • Ulcerated gums and mouth. The mercury family's mouth — spongy, ulcerated, bleeding gums.
  • Red blotches and coppery spots. The books record the red blotches and coppery skin-spots of the chapter.
  • Offensiveness of the discharges. The mercury offensiveness runs through the throat and the skin.
  • Worse cold damp and night. Wet weather and night deepen the throat and the skin.
  • Better for rest and moderate warmth. Rest and warmth ease the crimson state.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Throat and mouth, skin (blotches and spots), glands, general vitality.

Where the classical picture comes from

The crimson ore's proving gave the throat-and-skin portrait; Cinnabaris became the classical remedy of the syphilitic chapter's throat and blotches — the vermilion of the emperors serving the chapter the old shelf feared most.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Cinnabar is genuinely the mercury family's crimson twin: mercury sulfide, the pigment of scrolls and temples, its mercury real and regulated, its beauty undeniable and its toxicity documented. The syphilis it accompanied is cured with penicillin — one of medicine's absolute victories — and the ore's career belongs to art history and the toxicology shelf alike.
