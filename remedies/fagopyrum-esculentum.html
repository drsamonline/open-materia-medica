Fagopyrum

Made from buckwheat — the triangular seed of the high fields, its fagopyrin a genuine photosensitising chemistry, and the classical remedy of the itchy, prickling eczema.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of an itch that prickles beyond the rash: the eczema of the books with the peculiar prickling, itching worse for scratching, the blotched skin, and the triangular seed of the high fields. The prickling-itch seed: that is Fagopyrum.

The substance, in plain words

Buckwheat is the triangular seed of the high fields, its dark hulls carrying fagopyrin — a genuinely photosensitising chemistry documented in livestock as fagopyrism, the real toxicology of the grain's overuse. The proving gave the seed the prickling-itch portrait.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Anxiety about the skin. Worry over the itching, the blotches, the chronicity, worse at night.
  • Irritability. Cross from the constant prickling.
  • Despondency. Gloomy in the chronic states.

The body

  • Eczema with peculiar prickling. The keynote: the eczema with the peculiar, intolerable prickling itch of the books.
  • Itching worse for scratching. The quoted modality — the itch that scratching worsens.
  • Blotched skin. The blotched, uneven skin of the same chapter.
  • Worse cold damp and night. Wet weather and night drive the prickling wild.
  • Better for cool air and rest. Cool air and rest ease the buckwheat state.

Modalities (worse and better factors)

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Skin (eczema, pruritus), general vitality.

Where the classical picture comes from

The high-field seed's proving gave the prickling-itch portrait; Fagopyrum became the classical remedy of the eczema that prickles — the triangular grain serving the itch its own chemistry mirrors.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Buckwheat's fagopyrin is genuinely photosensitising — the documented fagopyrism of livestock fed too much buckwheat, a real, studied toxicology of the grain. The eczema it portrayed is now dermatology's; the seed's chemistry is real, its modern career the pancake and the soba noodle.
