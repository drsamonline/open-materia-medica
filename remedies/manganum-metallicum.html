Manganum

Made from manganese — the metal of the steel-works, an essential trace at the heart of the body's own antioxidant enzyme, and the classical remedy of the weather-sensitive chest.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of a chest that answers the weather: the chronic cough and hoarseness of the books, the chest and throat complaints that worsen as the barometer falls, the anaemic, weary frame, and the metal that arms the body's own defences. The weather-sensitive chest: that is Manganum.

The substance, in plain words

Manganese is the metal of the steel-works, hardening the alloys — and a genuinely essential trace element: it sits at the active centre of the body's own manganese superoxide dismutase, one of the antioxidant defences every cell runs on. The proving gave the metal the weather-sensitive portrait.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Anxiety about health. Worry over the chronic chest, the hoarseness, the weather's turns, worse at night.
  • Irritability. Cross from the chronic catarrhal drag.
  • Dullness. Heavy mind with the anaemic, weary frame.

The body

  • Weather-sensitive cough and hoarseness. The keynote: the chronic chest, cough and hoarseness that worsen with the weather's change — the quoted sensitivity.
  • Anaemic, weary frame. The books' portrait of the anaemic, exhausted constitution.
  • Chest and throat complaints. The chronic catarrhs of the chest and the hoarse throat of the same chapter.
  • Worse cold damp and weather change. Cold, wet and the falling barometer aggravate.
  • Better for rest and warmth. Rest and warmth ease the weather-sensitive chest.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Where the classical picture comes from

The steel-metal's proving gave the weather-sensitive portrait; Manganum became the classical remedy of the chest that answers the barometer — the trace element at the heart of the cell's defences serving the weather-watching chest.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Manganese is genuinely essential: it anchors the manganese superoxide dismutase — MnSOD — one of the body's own antioxidant enzymes, a real and important role in cellular defence. The weather-sensitive chest it portrayed is now respiratory medicine's; the metal's real story is the quiet indispensability of the enzyme it arms.
