Mezereum

Made from mezereon — the February daphne whose blistering bark was an official counter-irritant, and the classical remedy of crusted eruptions that burn under the crust.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of skin that burns beneath its own crust: thick, chalky, offensive crusts — scalp, face, eyebrows — under which the skin burns and itches maddeningly; worse at night, worse for the warmth of the bed, and the books' syphilitic-skin legacy all around. The burning-under-the-crust remedy: that is Mezereum.

The substance, in plain words

Mezereon is the purple-flowered shrub of late winter, its bark a violent blisterer — an official counter-irritant of the old pharmacopoeias, applied to raise blisters on purpose. Its daphnin and mezerein chemistry genuinely burns skin; the proving gave the classical crust-and-burn portrait.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Anxiety and restlessness. Fretful, worse at night with the itching; the books note the anxious skin-sufferer.
  • Irritability. Cross, worse from heat and from being questioned.
  • Despondency. Gloomy in the chronic crusted states.

The body

  • Thick chalky crusts. The keynote: thick, white-chalky, offensive crusts of the scalp, face and eyebrows; the hair sticks in them.
  • Burning under the crust. The skin beneath burns and itches — the quoted Mezereum sign; worse scratching
  • Worse warmth of the bed. Heat and the bed's warmth drive the itching wild; cool air relieves.
  • Zona and neuralgia. The books add it to the zona (shingles-like) and post-eruption neuralgias.
  • Night aggravation. The whole picture owns the night.
  • Syphilitic-skin legacy. The old books pair it with the inherited skin legacy of the era's great hidden disease.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Skin (crusted, burning), scalp and eyebrows, nerves (neuralgia), general constitution.

Where the classical picture comes from

The blistering bark's official career — blister-plasters on purpose — gave the proving its frame: burns beneath the crust, heat-madness, night-ownership. Mezereum became the classical crusted-skin remedy of the pre-steroid era.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Mezereon bark was a genuine, official counter-irritant of the pharmacopoeias — its blistering chemistry real and violent, since retired along with the whole blister-plaster era. The crusted skin diseases it portrayed — eczema, psoriasis, seborrhoea — are today dermatology's everyday territory, managed with steroid and vitamin-D creams.
