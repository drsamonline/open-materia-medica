Acidum nitricum

Made from nitric acid — the aqua fortis of the alchemists, whose fuming corrosion gave chemistry its name for a metal test, and the classical remedy of warts and bleeding points.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of a system that bleeds at the least touch: warts that crack and bleed, fissures that will not heal, ulcers with ragged edges and offensive discharge, stitching splinter-like pains, and the books' syphilitic-and-gouty legacy all at once. The bleeding-at-every-point remedy: that is Nitric acid.

The substance, in plain words

Nitric acid is aqua fortis — the alchemists' strong water, fuming, corrosive, dissolving copper with red fumes. It genuinely dissolves tissue — it is used to etch, to test, to clean — and its proving gave the library's portrait of bleeding, warty, fissured, corroded surfaces.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Anxiety about health. Fear of death, worse in the quiet hours; the books note the anxious, foreboding temperament.
  • Irritability. Cross, worse from noise and contradiction.
  • Despondency. Gloomy in the chronic, ragged, non-healing states.

The body

  • Warts that crack and bleed. The keynote: warts — moist, bleeding, at every orifice's edge — the books' great wart-and-bleeding remedy.
  • Fissures that won't heal. Cracks at the anus, the mouth's corners, the nipple — raw, smarting, unhealing.
  • Ulcers with ragged edges. Offensive, bleeding ulcers, the syphilitic legacy of the old books.
  • Splinter-like stitching pains. The acid's signature sensation — a splinter or fishbone in the part.
  • Offensive discharges and sweat. The body smells of the corroded state — sweat, urine, stool all offensive.
  • Worse cold damp and night. Wet weather and night aggravate every point.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Skin (warts, fissures, ulcers), mucous membranes, bones and periosteum, discharges.

Where the classical picture comes from

The acid's corrosion of the proving — bleeding points, ragged edges, splinter-pains — mapped onto the syphilitic-and-gouty casebooks of the era; Nitric acid became the remedy of surfaces that will not heal and points that will not stop bleeding.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Nitric acid's real chemistry is the laboratory's own: aqua fortis tests metals, etches glass, and dissolves the tissue it touches — a genuine, regulated corrosive. The conditions it portrayed — chronic ulcers, warts, bleeding — are today dermatology's territory, and the syphilis it accompanied is cured with penicillin, one of medicine's cleanest victories.

Safety. Any ulcer, fissure or wart that bleeds repeatedly, or will not heal for weeks, needs medical assessment — bleeding lesions are examined, not managed.
