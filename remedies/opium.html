Opium

Made from the poppy's latex — the source of morphine, humanity's oldest strong medicine, and the classical picture of the drowsy, dark, painless state.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of profound dullness: heavy, stupid sleep with stertorous breathing and a dark, bloated, dusky-red face; a body that feels no pain — the books' 'painlessness' — with pinpoint pupils and total insensibility. Heavy sleep, dark redness, insensibility: that is Opium.

The substance, in plain words

Opium is the dried latex of the opium poppy, source of morphine — the molecule that taught medicine what a strong analgesic is, and the ancestor of all opioids, blessing and curse of modern medicine. The homeopathic proving drew the picture of the poppy's stupor.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Stupefaction. The keynote: dull, heavy, stupid — vacant staring, answers nothing, sleeps through everything.
  • Fearful visions. In lighter states: frightful hallucinations, a sense of spectres; anxiety with the drowsiness.
  • Cheerful delirium. Alternating with silly, laughing, loquacious excitement — the two poppy moods.
  • Loss of fear. A strange indifference to danger; the 'painless and fearless' state of the books.

The body

  • Heavy, stertorous sleep. Deep, snoring, comatose sleep with slow breathing — the overdose picture par excellence.
  • Dark, dusky-red face. Dark red, bloated, hot face; blue lips; a 'congested' look unlike the pale collapse remedies.
  • Pinpoint pupils. The celebrated sign of the opiates — constricted pupils even in the dark.
  • Painlessness. Pain is felt late, little, or not at all — the books' strange keynote of the insensible state.
  • Constipation. Total inactivity of the bowel — the opiate grip that modern medicine still manages daily.
  • Worse for heat. Heat and hot coverings aggravate; the books note relief from cold and uncovering.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Brain and consciousness, breathing, bowel, circulation.

Where the classical picture comes from

The poppy's action was known from antiquity — sleep, insensibility, contracted pupils, constipation. The proving recorded the full stupor, and the remedy became the classical portrait of the heavy, dark, painless state — and of ailments from the drug itself, a problem the books already knew.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Morphine and its descendants are the foundation of pain medicine and anaesthesia — among the most important drug families in human history — and the modern opioid crisis shows the other face of the same molecule. Naloxone, the overdose antidote, saves lives by exactly reversing the portrait on this page. Few substances have taught medicine more, for good and ill.
