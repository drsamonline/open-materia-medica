Acidum salicylicum

Made from salicylic acid — the willow-bark chemistry that became aspirin, the world's most-used drug, and the classical remedy of rheumatism with ringing ears.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of rheumatism with deafness: wandering joint pains with sudden deafness or roaring in the ears, profuse sweating, vertigo, and the whole picture echoing the drug's own toxicology — because the drug and the remedy share one molecule's history. Rheumatism with ringing ears: that is Salicylic acid.

The substance, in plain words

Salicylic acid is the willow-bark chemistry that acetylated into aspirin in 1897 — the most-used drug in human history, antiplatelet, analgesic, antipyretic. Its own overdose — tinnitus, deafness, sweating, vertigo — is 'salicylism,' and the proving wrote the same portrait: rheumatism, ears, sweat.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Dullness and confusion. Foggy, confused, worse with the vertigo; delirium in fever states.
  • Anxiety. Worry with the ear-symptoms and the wandering pains.
  • Irritability. Cross, worse from noise — which the ears already ring with.

The body

  • Rheumatism with ear involvement. The keynote: wandering articular pains with sudden deafness or roaring tinnitus — the salicylate signature.
  • Profuse sweating. Drenching sweat with the pains and the fever.
  • Vertigo. Whirling vertigo, worse rising and moving.
  • Worse motion and night. The pains wander and worsen at night; motion aggravates the vertigo.
  • Hot dry skin alternating with sweat. The skin burns dry, then sweats profusely — the febrifuge legacy.
  • Better for rest. Stillness, quiet and warmth of the bed

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Joints (rheumatic wandering), ears and hearing, skin and sweat, head.

Where the classical picture comes from

The willow chemistry's toxicology — salicylism's ringing, deafness, sweating — wrote the proving before the drug existed to cause it; the remedy's rheumatism-ear portrait is a prophecy of aspirin's side-effect profile, decades early.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Salicylic acid's family is the greatest in over-the-counter medicine: aspirin — acetylsalicylic acid — prevents heart attacks and strokes at one dose, relieves pain and fever at another, and its precursor chemistry still treats warts and acne in topical form. Salicylate ototoxicity — the very tinnitus of the portrait — is taught in every pharmacology course. The molecule is medicine itself.
