Silicea

Made from flint — pure silicon dioxide, the quartz of crystals and sand; the classical remedy of weakness, cold sweat and stubborn pus.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of the fine-boned, delicate, chilly person with a weak spine and sweaty, offensive icy feet, who is yielding yet stubborn, whose abscesses heal slowly and whose body seems to 'expel' what doesn't belong — even splinters and glass. Head-sweats in children, fistulas that won't close, deficient vital heat. Weakness, sweat, slowness, expulsion: that is Silicea.

The substance, in plain words

Flint is quartz — silicon dioxide, the most abundant mineral of the earth's crust and an essential ultratrace element in human connective tissue. Ground and prepared, it became the classical 'chronic' remedy for poor vitality and slow repair — a portrait strikingly parallel to silicon's real structural roles in skin, bone and connective tissue.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Yielding obstinacy. The classical paradox: gentle, tearful, yielding — yet fixed and stubborn in resolve; the 'yielding silica child.'
  • Lack of grit. Easily discouraged, faint-hearted, dread of failure; offended by contradiction yet bows to it.
  • Concentration difficulty. The books note weak memory for words and absent-mindedness in study.

The body

  • The cold sweat of weakness. Profuse sweat of head, neck and feet — feet icy and offensive, head sweat wetting the pillow in sleep.
  • Weak spine and bones. A weak, curved, aching spine; slow bone repair; the classical rachitic companion of Calcarea.
  • Slow, stubborn abscesses. Abscesses and fistulas that refuse to heal; indurations; the books credit it with expelling foreign bodies — splinters, glass, even retained bullets of the era.
  • Chilliness. Intolerance of cold and damp; worse in winter; wants the head wrapped.
  • Nails and skin. Ingrowing nails, brittle nails, unhealthy scar tissue, chronic eczema.
  • Digestive weakness. Offensive stool, distended abdomen in children, desire for indigestible things, worse milk.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Bones, skin and connective tissue, glands (chronic), nervous system, nutrition.

Where the classical picture comes from

Silica's proving gave chilliness, head-sweat, weakness of spine and slow suppuration — matched clinically to the under-nourished, slow-healing children and chronic invalids of the pre-antibiotic and pre-vitamin era. Its 'foreign-body expulsion' reputation came from surgeons watching chronic wounds close after constitutional treatment.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Silicon is a genuine ultratrace nutrient: orthosilicic acid supports collagen synthesis, skin, hair, nails and bone mineralisation, and silica supplements are sold for connective-tissue health — though robust clinical evidence for supplementation is limited. Quartz itself is chemically inert. The chronic weakness and slow healing Silicea portrayed are today the territory of nutrition, endocrine and paediatric medicine.
