Staphysagria

Made from stavesacre seeds — the Delphinium whose seeds were sold for centuries as the natural lice poison, and the classical remedy of suppressed indignation.

The whole picture, in one breath

Think of the person who has swallowed an insult: quiet, refined, dignified — and seething. Ailments from humiliation and suppressed anger, styes and chalazions that keep returning, an irritable bladder set off by the slightest thing, skin that breaks out after indignation. Suppressed anger, recurring styes, irritable bladder: that is Staphysagria.

The substance, in plain words

Stavesacre is a tall Mediterranean Delphinium whose rough seeds were the standard natural insecticide and lice remedy of Europe for centuries — effective, but dangerously irritating to the skin. The seed's alkaloids and their old use give the remedy its characteristic 'vexation of the surface' picture.

The traditional picture Classical sources

The inner state

  • Suppressed indignation. The keynote: ailments from insults, humiliation, grief with held-back anger; the person is too dignified to explode.
  • Quiet resentment. A reserved, refined temperament; anger turned inward, indignation and humiliation
  • Hypersensitivity of feelings. Takes offence easily; the books describe a trembling indignation beneath the calm surface.

The body

  • Recurring styes and eyelid cysts. The famous use: stye after stye, chalazions that return — the 'vexation showing at the eyelid' of the classics.
  • Irritable bladder. Cystitis-like states after coition, urging from the slightest provocation, worse indignation.
  • Skin eruptions from anger. Eczema and itch that follow emotional wounds; skin worse touch and scratching.
  • Teeth and gums. Old-books use: blackening teeth, spongy bleeding gums, blackening of the teeth
  • Cutting abdominal pains. Colic after indignation; craving for tobacco
  • Worse from touch. The affected parts are touch-sensitive, like Hepar but with the emotional history of vexation.

Where it acts (organ affinities)

Eyelids and glands (styes), urinary tract, skin, teeth and gums, emotions.

Where the classical picture comes from

The seed's violently irritant alkaloids gave the proving its picture of irritated surfaces — eyelids, bladder, skin — and the emotional keynote of dignified vexation was drawn from the provers' states. The centuries of lice-powder use had already taught that this plant punishes contact.

The substance in real medicine Pharmacology

What this section is: the pharmacology and toxicology of the source substance. It is not clinical evidence that a homeopathic preparation of it treats any condition. What controlled trials do and do not show is set out on the Evidence page.

Stavesacre was the world's standard botanical pediculicide until safer insecticides arrived — a real, effective, if harsh medicine of the pre-DDT era. Its Delphinium alkaloids remain studied as insecticidal and neurotoxic compounds. The remedy's modern parallel is less pharmacological than psychological: the cost of swallowed anger is a live topic in psychosomatic medicine.
